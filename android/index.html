1. Introduction

DesignVerse ("we", "our", or "the App") is an educational interior design, 2D floor planning, and 3D spatial creativity application created for users of all ages, including children and families. We are firmly committed to protecting user privacy and adhering strictly to the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, Google Play Families Policy, COPPA, and GDPR.

2. Data Minimization & Information We Do Not Collect

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require, collect, or store real names, home addresses, emails, phone numbers, or social media logins.
  • No Location Data: We do not access, track, or collect GPS or precise geographical location data.
  • No Advertising IDs or Tracking: We do not access the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and do not conduct behavioral profiling, retargeting, or cross-app tracking.
  • No Sensitive Files: We never access personal contacts, SMS, phone states, or external photos beyond what you explicitly select for room scanning.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

A. Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)

Used strictly when you choose to photograph an interior space for 2D-to-3D room reconstruction or panorama generation. Images are processed transiently in memory for spatial extraction and are never sold, permanently saved on cloud servers, or shared with third parties. Camera access is completely optional.

B. Microphone & Audio (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)

Used solely for interactive voice commands (e.g., "make wall blue", "add chair") and speech interactions with Sparky the AI Tutor. Audio is processed transiently for intent recognition and is never continuously recorded, stored permanently, or shared with third-party advertisers.

C. Internet (android.permission.INTERNET)

Used to sync optional learning progress and download design assets. All network communications are encrypted with standard HTTPS (TLS 1.3).

4. Children's Privacy & Families Policy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Because DesignVerse is designed for children and families:

  • Zero Third-Party Advertising: The app contains NO behavioral advertising SDKs, banner ads, or interstitials.
  • No Public Broadcasts: Children's 3D rooms and plans are stored locally on the device and cannot be shared publicly to unmoderated networks.
  • Child-Safe AI Tutor: Sparky operates within strict educational architectural boundaries and never solicits personal data.
  • Parental Gate: Administrative controls, progress resets, and external links are locked behind an interactive math verification challenge.

6. User Data Deletion Rights

Parents and users can delete all saved rooms and progress at any time directly in the app via the Parent Area, or through Android Settings (Apps → DesignVerse → Storage → Clear Data). To request deletion of any cloud-synced records, email support@designverse.app.
