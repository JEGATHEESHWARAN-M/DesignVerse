# 🎮 DesignVerse Kids — Activity Engine

## 1. Reusable Activity Types
The activity engine provides 12 specialized activity handlers:
1. `ObjectIdentification` — Locate and tap specific room parts in 3D (floor, door, window).
2. `ObjectMatching` — Connect furniture pieces to their primary functions.
3. `ColorSelection` — Apply warm, cool, or calm color palettes.
4. `ColorMatching` — Pair complementary colors for room harmony.
5. `ObjectPlacement` — Place a piece of furniture into a designated spot.
6. `ObjectMovement` — Move items away from obstructions.
7. `Measurement` — Visually compare room width (m) to furniture footprint.
8. `DoorClearance` — Ensure 0.9m swing corridor is open.
9. `RoomArrangement` — Coordinate bed, desk, and lamp in a full room.
10. `FloorPlanDrawing` — Draw walls and place doors on a 2D architectural grid.
11. `3DExploration` — First-person walkthrough of the built room.
12. `AIChallenge` — Prompt Sparky to suggest creative styling ideas.
