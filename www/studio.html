🔒 Privacy Policy & Data Safety

DesignVerse respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in full compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies.

1. Camera Access (ANDROID.PERMISSION.CAMERA)

Used strictly to capture interior room photographs for real-time 3D reconstruction. Images are processed locally on your device or temporary session memory and are never sold or shared with third parties.

2. Microphone & Audio (RECORD_AUDIO)

Used solely to receive natural speech voice commands (e.g. "change wall color to blue", "add modern sofa"). Audio input is processed transiently for intent recognition and is not permanently recorded or transmitted.

3. Local Device Storage

Used to save and load your custom floor plans, 3D project states, and exported high-resolution renders locally on your device.

4. Data Security

All stored data is kept isolated within the application container. No user tracking or behavioral advertising profiling is performed.
