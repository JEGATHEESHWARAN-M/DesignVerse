# 📚 DesignVerse Kids — Complete 10-Level Curriculum

## UNIT 1 — 🌱 MY ROOM (Level 1)
- **Concepts:** What is a room, Floor, Walls, Ceiling, Door, Window.
- **Interactive Activity:** Tap room components in the live 3D room.
- **Mini-Game:** Find It Detective (Locate window, door, and floor).
- **Tool Unlocks:** Room exploration, Object selection, Simple camera guidance.

## UNIT 2 — 🪑 FURNITURE FRIENDS (Level 2)
- **Concepts:** Chair, Table, Bed, Sofa, Storage, Desk, Lamp.
- **Interactive Activity:** Add a chair and place it near the dining table.
- **Mini-Game:** Furniture Match-Up (Item to purpose matching).
- **Tool Unlocks:** Furniture catalog, Add furniture, Move furniture.

## UNIT 3 — 🎨 COLOR WORLD (Level 3)
- **Concepts:** Primary colors, secondary colors, warm vs cool, light vs dark moods.
- **Interactive Activity:** Real-time 3D wall and floor recoloring.
- **Mini-Game:** Color Mood Explorer (Calm bedrooms vs Happy play areas).
- **Tool Unlocks:** Color swatches, Wall colors, Furniture recoloring.

## UNIT 4 — 🔷 SHAPES & SPACE (Level 4)
- **Concepts:** Shapes in furniture (rectangles, circles, squares), near/far, left/right, front/behind.
- **Interactive Activity:** Positioning bed against the wall.
- **Mini-Game:** Shape Puzzle matching.
- **Tool Unlocks:** Move objects, Basic rotate, Resize elements.

## UNIT 5 — 📐 SMART SPACE (Level 5)
- **Concepts:** Walking space, door swing clearance, measurements, room proportions.
- **Interactive Activity:** Moving blocking chair away from the doorway corridor.
- **Mini-Game:** Clear Path Detective (Spatial obstruction solver).
- **Tool Unlocks:** Grid snap, Collision warnings, Measurement markers.

## UNIT 6 — 🏠 MY FIRST ROOM DESIGN (Level 6)
- **Concepts:** Bedroom, Living Room, Study Room, complete room layout.
- **Interactive Activity:** Assembling a multi-piece room with matched colors.
- **Challenge:** Child Bedroom Challenge (Bed + Table + Chair + Storage).
- **Tool Unlocks:** Full Room Builder, Room types, Decorations.

## UNIT 7 — 💡 DESIGN EXPLORER (Level 7)
- **Concepts:** Balance, contrast, focal points, cozy lighting, natural materials (wood, fabric, stone, metal).
- **Interactive Activity:** Applying wood flooring and adjusting room lighting modes.
- **Mini-Game:** Warm Lighting and Material matcher.
- **Tool Unlocks:** Material selector, Daylight/Cozy Lighting modes.

## UNIT 8 — 🗺️ FLOOR PLAN EXPLORER (Level 8)
- **Concepts:** Bird's-eye map view, walls, doors, window cutouts, furniture symbols.
- **Interactive Activity:** Drawing 2D room boundaries on the grid canvas.
- **Challenge:** Bedroom floor plan drawing.
- **Tool Unlocks:** 2D Floor Plan creator, Wall drawing tool, Furniture stamps.

## UNIT 9 — 🌍 2D TO 3D MAGIC (Level 9)
- **Concepts:** How flat maps turn into real volumetric 3D rooms via AI geometry & scene graphs.
- **Interactive Activity:** Reconstructing 2D floor plans into 3D walkthrough rooms.
- **Tool Unlocks:** 2D-to-3D converter, 3D walkthrough mode.

## UNIT 10 — 👑 YOUNG DESIGNER (Level 10)
- **Concepts:** Complete end-to-end interior design planning, styling, and portfolio creation.
- **Final Project:** "Design Your Dream Room" Masterpiece.
- **Graduation:** Multi-criteria evaluation + Young Designer Diploma.
- **Tool Unlocks:** ALL DESIGN TOOLS, Master Graduation Badge.
