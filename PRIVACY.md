# 🔒 DesignVerse — Privacy Policy & Child Data Safety

Please refer to the complete, production-grade Play Store Privacy Policy in [`PLAY_STORE_PRIVACY_POLICY.md`](PLAY_STORE_PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

## Quick Summary
- **No Personal Identifiers Collected:** No names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords required.
- **Children's Privacy (COPPA / Google Play Families Policy):**
  - Strict data minimization.
  - Zero third-party ad tracking or behavioral advertising SDKs.
  - Safe conversational AI tutor restricted to architecture & interior design pedagogy.
  - Protected adult/parent area with interactive math gate.
- **Permissions:**
  - `CAMERA`: Ephemeral spatial scanning for room photos; never stored permanently or shared.
  - `RECORD_AUDIO`: Transient voice command interpretation; no audio saved or transmitted to advertisers.
  - `INTERNET`: Secure HTTPS data sync and asset delivery.
- **Data Deletion:**
  - In-app 1-tap progress and room deletion via Parental Area.
  - System-level deletion via Android Application Storage settings.
  - Support contact: support@designverse.app
