# 🏆 DesignVerse Kids — Skill Mastery System (15-Skill DAG)

## 1. Mastery DAG Architecture
The skill mastery system is organized as a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) with explicit prerequisite dependencies.

```
🌱 Room Basics (L1)
  ├── 🪑 Furniture Friends (L2) ──> 🔷 Shapes in Rooms (L4) ──> 📐 Walking Space (L5)
  │                                                                 ├── 🚶 Smart Placement (L5) ──> 🏠 Full Room Planning (L6)
  │                                                                 └── 📏 Room Sizes (L5)                  │
  └── 🎨 Color Magic (L3) ──> 🪵 Cozy Materials (L7) ──> 💡 Sunlight & Lamps (L7)                         │
                                      │                                                                      │
                                      └──────────────────> ✨ Balance & Style (L7) <─────────────────────────┘
                                                                 │
                                                       🗺️ Bird's-Eye Maps (L8)
                                                                 │
                                                       🌍 3D World Building (L9) ──> 🤖 AI Design Helper (L9)
                                                                 │
                                                       👑 Young Designer Master (L10)
```

## 2. 15 Definitive Design Skills

1. `room_basics` — What is a room, floors, walls, doors, and windows.
2. `furniture_basics` — Functions of chairs, tables, beds, and storage.
3. `color_basics` — Warm, cool, and balanced colors for comfort.
4. `shape_basics` — Identifying 2D and 3D shapes inside furniture.
5. `space_basics` — Leaving walking paths and avoiding doorway blocks.
6. `furniture_placement` — Smart placement relative to windows and walls.
7. `measurement_basics` — Understanding room size and object proportions.
8. `room_planning` — Coordinating a full bedroom, living, or study room.
9. `materials_basics` — Wood, fabric, stone, glass, and metal properties.
10. `lighting_basics` — Daylight, ambient lighting, and cozy bedtime lamps.
11. `design_principles` — Visual balance, focal points, and harmony.
12. `floor_plan_basics` — Drawing architectural maps seen from above.
13. `3d_design` — Spatial volumetric modeling in 3D.
14. `ai_design` — Prompting AI assistants to modify and style rooms.
15. `young_designer_master` — Complete portfolio mastery and dream room design.
