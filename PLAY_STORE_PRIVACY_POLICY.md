## 1. Introduction
DesignVerse ("we", "our", or "the App") is an educational interior design, 2D floor planning, and 3D spatial creativity application created for users of all ages, including children and families.

We are firmly committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices, permissions usage, and compliance with the **Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement**, **Google Play Families Policy**, the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
We follow a strict **Data Minimization Principle**. When using DesignVerse:
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not require, collect, or store real names, physical addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, or social media logins.
- **No Precise Location:** We do not access, track, or collect GPS or precise geographical location data.
- **No Advertising IDs or Tracking:** We do not access Android Advertising ID (AAID) and do not perform behavioral user profiling, retargeting, or cross-app tracking.
- **No Contact Lists or Sensitive Files:** We never access contacts, text messages, phone state, or unrelated device folders.

## 3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

DesignVerse requests only the runtime permissions necessary to deliver core educational and creative features:

### A. Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`)
- **Purpose:** Allows the user to take a photograph of an interior room to generate a 3D floor layout or 360° room panorama.
- **Processing:** Captured images are processed transiently in local device memory or temporary active session memory solely for computer vision spatial geometry extraction.
- **Storage & Sharing:** Images are **never** sold, permanently stored on external servers, or shared with third parties.
- **Optional:** The camera is strictly optional (`android.hardware.camera required="false"`). The app is fully functional using built-in room templates, manual dimensions, and the 2D floor planner without camera access.

### B. Microphone & Audio (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`)
- **Purpose:** Used strictly for natural language voice commands (e.g., *"Make the wall blue"*, *"Add a chair"*, *"What is a room?"*) and interactive voice learning with Sparky the AI Tutor.
- **Processing:** Audio is processed transiently in real-time via local or secure speech-to-text recognition solely for parsing intent.
- **Storage & Sharing:** Audio is **never** continuously recorded, never stored as permanent audio files, and never shared with advertising networks or third parties.

### C. Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET`)
- **Purpose:** Enables optional synchronization of learning progress with the secure backend API, downloading optional 3D asset packs, and standard network diagnostics.
- **Security:** All network communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS (TLS 1.3).

## 4. Children’s Privacy & Families Policy Compliance (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Because DesignVerse includes **DesignVerse Kids** for young learners (ages 6–14):

1. **Child-Safe Content:** All 3D assets, furniture, textures, and educational lessons are strictly non-violent, age-appropriate, and family-friendly.
2. **Zero Third-Party Advertising:** The application contains **NO third-party advertisements**, no banner ads, no interstitial ads, and no behavioral ad SDKs.
3. **No Social Broadcasts:** Children’s created 3D rooms and floor plans are stored in private local device storage and cannot be publicly broadcast to public unmoderated feeds.
4. **Child-Safe AI Tutor (Sparky):** Sparky is strictly constrained to educational interior design topics (spatial planning, walking paths, colors, lighting, and materials). The AI mentor never requests personal details and never engages in unmoderated conversations.
5. **Parental Gate:** Access to administrative settings, data reset options, and external links is protected by an interactive math challenge barrier to prevent accidental actions by young children.

---

## 5. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

- **Local Storage:** Your saved rooms, custom floor plans, learning levels, stars, and XP progress are stored locally within the application's sandboxed storage on your device.
- **Data Deletion:**
  - **In-App:** Parents can instantly wipe all saved learning progress and custom rooms at any time via the **Parent Area** in the app.
  - **Device Settings:** You can permanently delete all locally stored data at any time by going to **Android Settings → Apps → DesignVerse → Storage → Clear Data**, or by uninstalling the app.
  - **Support Request:** To request deletion of any optional cloud-synced account data, email **support@designverse.app** with your User ID, and data will be permanently purged within 30 days.
