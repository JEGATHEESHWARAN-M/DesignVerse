# 🌍 DesignVerse Kids — 3D Spatial & Object Learning

## 1. Real 3D Objects & Spatial Interaction
Every room component and furniture asset in DesignVerse Kids is a real 3D volumetric object (Three.js Mesh / Unity GameObject), never a flat fake image.

## 2. Progressive 3D Control by Level
- **Level 1 (My Room):** Automatic guided camera orbits smoothly around room elements. Single-tap selection highlights the targeted floor, door, or window.
- **Level 2–4 (Furniture & Shapes):** Drag-and-drop 3D furniture placement with bounding-box collision feedback.
- **Level 5 (Smart Space):** Snapping grid, real collision detection, and red glowing warning fields on blocked doorways.
- **Level 7 (Materials & Lighting):** Live PBR shader updates for warm natural wood, fabric, and soft daylight illumination.
- **Level 9–10 (Young Designer):** Complete 3D first-person walkthrough with dynamic camera orbit and 360° panorama mode.
