# 🛡️ DesignVerse Kids — Child Safety & Protection Guidelines

## 1. Compliance with Child App Standards
DesignVerse Kids complies with Google Play Families Policy, COPPA, and international children's digital safety regulations:
- **No Inappropriate Content:** All assets, furniture, textures, and AI responses are strictly curated for general audiences (all ages).
- **No Behavioral Advertising:** The app contains zero third-party ad networks, trackers, or behavioral profiling.
- **No Open Social Sharing:** Children's created rooms are saved locally on the device and cannot be broadcast to public unmoderated feeds.
- **Parental Gate:** Access to settings, data deletion, and adult areas is protected by a randomized math verification barrier.
